1) Three directions

A. Wet Paper
Mood: a Tel Aviv studio in the late afternoon. Quiet, tactile, a little unfinished. The paintings are louder than the site.
Palette: #F3EBDF paper, #1C1917 charcoal, #B4322A red, #1F4E9A cobalt, #C6A15A ochre, #8C6A4A umber.
Type: Frank Ruhl Libre for display, Heebo for body and UI. Both are the real Hebrew faces, not a Latin font with a fallback.
Devices: brush-stroke rules instead of boxes; a faint paper grain; dot pattern only behind colour search, very light; paintings bleed off the left edge; titles sit hard to the right. One ochre dot as the hover, nothing else moving.
Sections: hero is a full-bleed work with two short lines over the paper margin, not a banner. The statement is the poem set as separate lines, large, right-aligned, lots of air, slashes removed visually only. The 3D room is the one warm plaster room, afternoon light, not a black museum. Gallery is an uneven RTL masonry of true paper and canvas proportions, small captions, no card chrome. Colour search is large pigment swatches. “On my wall” uses three plain local walls: warm plaster, white, one deep blue. About is a short column. Contact is a quiet form, WhatsApp first.

B. Dot Dream
Mood: nocturnal, surreal, a little naive. Faces almost appear, then don’t.
Palette: #0C1020 night, #E7DCCB bone, #E23B2F red, #2F5BD0 cobalt, #D7A45A ochre, #7A6A86 dusty violet.
Type: Secular One for short display lines, Assistant for body.
Devices: paintings float with a soft halo; dotted fields as section breaks; circle-and-dot marks only as empty states, never as fake paintings.
Sections: the round room is the homepage. Gallery is a dark field, works as glowing rectangles, reading right to left. Colour search is a night sky of dots you tap. “On my wall” is a dim domestic wall, one lamp. Statement in bone type on night. Risk: the interface starts competing with the pictures.

C. Sun Wall
Mood: noon, editorial, grown-up. Clear and confident, almost a print shop.
Palette: #F6F3EE, #111111, #D21F1A, #163F9A, #E4C99A, #2F3D34.
Type: Bellefair for display, Heebo for body.
Devices: one huge cropped Hebrew word behind the hero; a strict grid that breaks only for a full-bleed work; hairline frames; pigment blocks instead of filter chips.
Sections: white, spacious, prices and labels very small. Gallery is a calm RTL grid, then one work takes the full width. Colour search is a row of flat swatches. The 3D room and wall preview sit in simple frames, like plates in a book. Risk: if the type is not big enough, it collapses back into a nice template.

2) Recommendation

Use Wet Paper, and borrow Sun Wall’s pigment swatches for colour search. His statement is about the physical second a work starts, brush marks, and a quieter place. A generic template makes the paintings into products. A dark dotted theme will fight the dots and faces already in the work. Paper, charcoal, and real pigment let the reds and cobalts shout, and leave the 3D room as the single immersive moment. Keep the whole UI in Hebrew RTL: dir=rtl, logical spacing, gallery starts on the right, arrows point left.
